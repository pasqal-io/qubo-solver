    Analyzing QUBO solutions¶

    When using a solver, we return a Solution instance. It is composed of candidate solutions presented as bitstrings, with their respective QUBO evaluation. If a quantum approach was used, we also save counts or frequencies obtained from sampling from the quantum device , as well as the probabilities (frequencies divided by the total number of samples).

    To analyze the solutions from one or many QUBO solvers, we can use Analyzer. This will generate a pandas dataframe internally (accessible via the df attribute) for several Analyzer methods for plotting or comparing solutions. Here we show how to compare two solutions (generated randomly for simplicity of the tutorial).
